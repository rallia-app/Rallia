# Rallia — Monetization Strategy

_One-pager · April 2026_

---

## Strategic thesis

Rallia is a platform business disguised as a consumer app. A single matchmaking product attracts **six distinct payer types**, each monetizable independently, each feeding the others. Long-term defensibility comes from _stacking_ these streams — not from any one of them.

---

## Six payer types, one product

| Payer          | Why they pay                                          | Primary stream           |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| **Players**    | Efficiency, access, status                            | Plus subscription        |
| **Organizers** | Tools to run leagues & tournaments                    | Pro tier                 |
| **Coaches**    | Booking infrastructure + student management           | Commission + coach sub   |
| **Facilities** | Demand, visibility, utilization insight               | Promoted listings + SaaS |
| **Brands**     | Reach into the fastest-growing racket-sports audience | Sponsorship + affiliate  |
| **Companies**  | Employee wellness & engagement                        | Bulk Plus seats          |

---

## Revenue streams

### Recurring — the ARR backbone

- **Plus** — $7.99/mo · $59.99/yr · Household $11.99/mo
- **Pro / Organizer** — $19.99/mo · $149/yr (or per-event)
- **Rallia for Coaches** — $29/mo · $249/yr
- **Verified Coach badge** — $99/yr
- **Facility SaaS** — $99–499/mo (phase 4)
- **Corporate Plus seats** — bulk volume pricing

### Transactional — scales with GMV

- **Coach booking commission** — 10–15%
- **Cost-splitting fee** (free users only) — $0.50 per share
- **Facility booking affiliate** — 3–10%
- **Equipment affiliate** — 3–10% (Amazon, Tennis Warehouse, Selkirk, etc.)
- **Travel / destination commission** — 5–15%

### One-time & event-based

- **Expert-verified rating** — ~$25
- **Tournament hosting** — $5–10 per event (alternative to Pro sub)
- **Sponsored ladders & tournaments** — $2–50K per deal
- **Rallia-hosted events** — ticketing + sponsors
- **Gift cards / prepaid Plus credits**

### Performance & ads — late-stage

- Promoted coach listings
- Promoted facility placement
- Sponsored content / brand activations
- Anonymized data insights for brands & operators

## Phased roadmap

| Phase                         | Focus                                                    | Streams unlocked                                                                   |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **1 · Prove pay-willingness** | Ship Plus, basic splitting, affiliate links              | Plus, Household, splitting fee, equipment affiliate                                |
| **2 · Unlock power users**    | Pro tier, expert verifier network, facility partnerships | Pro, expert verification, promoted facilities, Rallia-hosted events, gift cards    |
| **3 · Launch marketplaces**   | Coach marketplace, brand partnerships, corporate sales   | Coach commission, coach sub, verified coach, sponsorships, corporate seats, travel |
| **4 · Platform scale**        | Facility SaaS, data products, licensing                  | SaaS, data insights, rating licensing, promoted listings, ad products              |

Sequencing is deliberate: each phase validates the next and feeds the following one's cold-start problem. _Plus proves pay-willingness → expert verification seeds coach supply → coaches bring their students in as new free users who fuel Plus._

## Guardrails

1. **Free must stay liquid.** Never paywall core matchmaking — paid tiers are worthless without active supply. The free experience _is_ the demand engine.
2. **Sequence the flywheels.** Parallel builds double the cold-start problem. Plus → verification → coaches → facilities → brands, in that order.
3. **One product, six payers.** Every stream must ride on the core matchmaking product. Resist pivoting into standalone booking SaaS or coach CRM.
4. **Prioritize ARR > transactional > one-time.** Recurring compounds; transactional scales with GMV; one-time is opportunistic revenue, not a foundation.
5. **Non-custodial until it isn't.** Stay out of money-transmitter territory — use Stripe Connect destination charges for splitting and coach bookings so Stripe remains processor of record.
